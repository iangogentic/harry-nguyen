Case study 01 · GoGogiri, 2021 – 2024

The
weekly
drop

How we sold Korean corn dogs like sneakers.

Scroll →

01

Drop

One new flavor, every week. Limited, and gone when it's gone. The menu became the reason to come back.

02

Film

A new flavor is a new video. We never needed a content calendar because the product was the calendar.

03

Post

Organic first on Instagram and TikTok. Then paid spend on Meta, Google and TikTok behind whatever was already working.

04

Sell out

Demand got big enough that corn dogs earned their own dedicated truck. Local radio covered the launch.

05

Repeat

Read what sold, read the comments, pick next week's flavor. Same loop, run again.

What it built

  • 5locations opened across Lubbock, Abilene and Dallas
  • $175Krevenue in the best month
  • 5.2Mviews across three TikTok accounts
  • 17Kfollowers on the Dallas Instagram alone

Then the hard lesson: attention is the easy part. Restaurant margins are the hard part.
